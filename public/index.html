    How the numbers are worked out
    • A year is 52 weeks. Money is rounded to the nearest cent. Yields and returns are rounded to two decimal places.
    • Low rent uses the lowest weekly rent field. High rent uses the highest weekly rent field. Mid rent is halfway between them.
    • Gross yield is annual rent divided by the purchase price. Annual rent is weekly rent times 52. Vacancy and costs are not deducted. This matches the gross yield quoted on most New Zealand listing sites.
    • Fixed annual costs are council rates, insurance, body corporate fees, water rates, maintenance and repairs, and other annual costs.
    • The vacancy allowance is weekly rent times the weeks vacant. The property management fee is your percentage of the rent collected in the other weeks.
    • Total annual operating costs are the fixed costs, the vacancy allowance and the management fee. Net operating income is annual rent minus those costs.
    • Net yield is net operating income divided by the purchase price. The mortgage is not taken off in the net yield.
    • The loan is the purchase price minus the deposit. A percent deposit is that percent of the purchase price. Cash invested is the deposit plus legal fees, the building inspection and other one-off costs.
    • Cash-on-cash return is annual cash flow after the mortgage, divided by cash invested, before tax. Income tax is not calculated.
    • Principal and interest is a standard monthly repayment over the loan term. The annual repayment is the monthly amount times 12. The weekly repayment is the annual amount divided by 52. Interest only is one year of interest on the loan, and does not use the loan term.
    • Weekly cash flow is net operating income minus the annual mortgage, divided by 52.
    • Break-even weekly rent is the rent that makes cash flow zero. It is the same on every card.
    • The price for your target gross yield is annual rent divided by that yield. The target starts at 5%. Each rent has its own price.
    • Enter rent as the amount the tenant pays. Residential rent in New Zealand is generally exempt from GST. If a manager adds GST to their fee, include it in the percentage.
